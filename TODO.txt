🔴 Critical Basics
  ~1. basics-linear-regression - Linear regression from scratch (MSE loss, gradient descent, analytical solution)
  ~2. basics-loss-functions - Compare MSE, MAE, Cross-Entropy, Hinge Loss with visualizations
  ~3. basics-optimizers - SGD, Momentum, Adam, RMSprop side-by-side comparison
  4. basics-learning-rate-schedules - Step decay, exponential decay, cosine annealing, warmup
  5. basics-regularization - L1/L2 regularization, dropout, early stopping, weight decay
  6. basics-data-augmentation - Image augmentation techniques (rotation, flip, crop, color jitter)

  ~7. basics-embeddings - Word embeddings, character embeddings, positional encodings

  ~10. basics-feature-engineering - Normalization, standardization, one-hot encoding, binning
  ~11. basics-overfitting-underfitting - Visualization with polynomial regression and model capacity

  15. autoencoder-basics - Simple autoencoder for dimensionality reduction
  16. vae-from-scratch - Variational autoencoder with latent space visualization
  17. gan-from-scratch - Simple GAN for generating simple images (MNIST)

  19. basics-evaluation-metrics - Precision, recall, F1, ROC-AUC, confusion matrix
